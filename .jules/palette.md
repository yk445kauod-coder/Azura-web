## 2025-03-24 - [Premium Animated Typography]
**Learning:** Manual typing effects using `setInterval` are fragile, difficult to sync with other UI transitions, and often feel "robotic". Using `framer-motion` for character-level staggered animations (scale, blur, rotate) creates a significantly more premium "Summer Edition" handwritten feel while being easier to maintain and more accessible to the browser's animation loop.
**Action:** Replace imperative text timers with declarative staggered character animations for hero/splash sections.
